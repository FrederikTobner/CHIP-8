◆ PROGRAM_START_LOCATION

#define PROGRAM_START_LOCATION   (0x200u)

Most Chip-8 programs start at location 0x200 (512), but some begin at 0x600 (1536). Programs beginning at 0x600 are intended for the ETI 660 computer. Our emulator will only support programs starting at 0x200 for now.
